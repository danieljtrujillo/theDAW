What was measured
  The Detmold Konzerthaus (about 600 seats, 4600 m3) with a loudspeaker
  orchestra of eight Neumann KH120 sources on stage and a Neumann KU100 dummy
  head at audience seat 211 (centre of the front of the fixed stalls) and seat
  372 (centre of the rear stalls). Seen from the audience, S1-S4 are the front
  row from left to right and S5-S8 the row 2 m behind it.

Changes made (scripts/build_hall_irs.py)
  - Each seat's eight responses are trimmed to start 1.5 ms before the earliest
    direct sound among them, scaled by one gain per seat, faded out over their
    last 50 ms and written as 16-bit FLAC at 48 kHz.
  - s1.flac-s8.flac are sources S1-S8. stage.flac is new: the mean of the eight
    responses, scaled on its own, standing for the whole stage playing at once.
